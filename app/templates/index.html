Credit Card Fraud Finder

Fraud Finder scores a single card transaction and estimates the probability that it is fraudulent. It is trained on the Kaggle "Credit Card Transactions Fraud Detection" (Sparkov) dataset: about 1.85 million simulated transactions from 2019–2020.

  • Inputs: amount, time, merchant category, customer and merchant location, and recent activity on the card
  • Output: fraud probability, plus a flag/allow decision at a tuned threshold
